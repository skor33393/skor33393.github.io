A few useful details.

Is it free to download?

Yes. Stylist AI is free to download, with in-app purchases. Some features require a subscription. Check current pricing and offers in the app.

Is the passport above my personal result?

No. It is an example showing what a color passport looks like. Use your own photo in Stylist AI for your personal analysis. The decorative mesh on this page uses a sample portrait.

What photo works best?

Use a clear selfie in natural light, with your face visible. Lighting and camera settings can affect your result.
