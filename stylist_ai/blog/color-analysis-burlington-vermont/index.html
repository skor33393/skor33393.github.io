Make the arrival plan explicit

Ask for arrival instructions, the current total and any access accommodations you need. Confirm whether the booking is for color analysis alone or also includes style advice. If you are bringing garments or makeup, ask how many items are practical within the session. Avoid relying on an old map listing for a private appointment address.

  • What are the current arrival and accessibility arrangements?
  • Which services and take-home references are included in my booking?

Connect the consultation to a real clothing decision

If travel or access makes the visit difficult, you can begin exploring colors on your phone at home. Use even light and one familiar outfit, then write down the questions a live consultant might help answer. An app offers convenience; an in-person session offers discussion and physical draping. The decision can be about access and learning style as much as the palette itself.
