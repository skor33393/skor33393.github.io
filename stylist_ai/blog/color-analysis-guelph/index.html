Separate regular pricing, promotions and delivered materials

For colour analysis in Guelph, check both the regular package and any temporary promotion. A discount can be useful, but it should not obscure when you receive your materials or whether the group price is per person. Compare the final amount and deliverables in writing.

Posy House Styling

Put the material-delivery date beside your event date

Ask when the swatch book arrives and what you can use immediately after the appointment. If bringing makeup or natural-hair photos, follow the provider’s preparation instructions. For a group, confirm that every guest receives the same references and enough individual discussion. Do not assume that a virtual option is bookable when the website still describes it as coming soon.

  • What is the final price after HST and any currently valid promotion?
  • When will my swatch book arrive, and what do I receive on the day?

Keep group pricing separate from an individual offer

Use the waiting period for any delivered materials to build a small outfit plan from existing clothes. Choose a neutral, two accents and one accessory, then note which combinations feel easy to wear. Stylist AI can help you explore those ideas on your phone. A live appointment adds physical comparison and conversation; choose it when that is the kind of support you want.

Use the waiting period for a small wardrobe experiment

If the book arrives after the purchase you are planning, ask what the immediate card can help you do in the meantime.
