THE DUET DECISION

Huntsville color analysis for two: divide the price, then compare the service

Twelve Season Studio lists a US$175 solo session and a US$300 duet. Splitting the duet makes it US$150 each, but that arithmetic does not tell you how the time or services are shared.

US$300 total÷2 guests=US$150 each

The listed solo duration is 1.5–2 hours; the duet is 2.5–3 hours for the whole booking. Dividing a total is not evidence of equal private consultation time.

Compare group prices per person and per service

Huntsville group bookings show why a lower per-person price needs a scope check. Two people may share an appointment without receiving the same services in every package. Compare the individual attention and materials before deciding that a pair session is better value.

Twelve Season Studio

The menu lists a solo session at US$175 for 1.5–2 hours and a duet at US$300 total for two people over 2.5–3 hours. The duet therefore works out to US$150 each. It also lists a different date package whose hair and makeup component is not identical for both guests.

A duet and a date package are not interchangeable

Write down the total price, divide it by the actual number of guests, and list what each person receives. Ask whether both guests get full draping, makeup advice, a fan and written guidance. Clarify the cancellation policy if one person cannot attend. A shared appointment is most useful when both people want the experience and have enough time for their own questions.

  • Does each person receive the same analysis and materials?
  • What happens to the group price if one guest cancels?

Use the shared explanation without sharing a palette

If only one of you is curious, start individually with a phone palette and a small outfit experiment. If both want human feedback, a duet may make sense. Afterwards, keep your references separate; sharing a shopping trip does not mean sharing the same palette. Choose one new combination each and try it with clothes you already own before buying more.

Together in the studio, independently in the app

Ask what each participant receives, particularly if you are choosing the separately described date package. Its hair and makeup components are not identical for both guests. It is reasonable for two people to want different amounts of guidance, but the difference should be understood before reserving.

In Stylist AI, each person starts with their own selfie and color profile. Outfit combinations, makeup or hair previews and the camera comparison can then be explored independently. The app is useful when you want individual experimentation without synchronising calendars. A duet is more appropriate when hearing the explanations together is part of the experience you want. Neither route means two friends should adopt the same colors.
