Put your palette into an outfit

Keep the clothes that work for your life. Choose one outfit problem, then use color to help solve that specific problem.

  1. Choose a small wardrobe palette

    How to build a wardrobe color palette you will actually use

    Start with your worn outfits, not an empty shopping list. Your palette should make those outfits easier to repeat.

  2. Keep personal style at the office

    How can I keep my personal style in a casual office?

    Build a casual work wardrobe without defaulting to jeans. Try three outfit formulas, clarify the dress code and use your existing clothes first.

  3. Choose shoes for a walking commute

    What can I wear instead of sneakers for a walking commute?

    Compare lace-up shoes, low boots and strapped flats, then test fit and plan colors around the trousers you actually wear.

  4. Compare greens for a softer palette

    Which green should I try with a Soft Summer palette?

    Compare olive, blue-green, sage and teal without treating a color name as a verdict. Use a three-pair fabric check and two everyday outfit examples.

  5. Care for dark denim

    How can I keep dark-wash jeans dark for longer?

    Build a care plan for dark jeans, distinguish fading from dye transfer, and decide whether to restyle or recolor a pair you still like.

  6. Choose a layer under a blouse

    What can I wear under a blouse without the neckline showing?

    Choose blouse underlayers by neckline, sleeve coverage and visibility. Compare scoop-neck tees, deep-V layers and camisoles before buying multiples.

  7. Try relaxed petite proportions

    Can petite outfits have a loose top and wide trousers?

    Try loose tops with wide trousers without rigid petite styling rules. Compare an untucked shirt, a small front tuck and an open layer using clothes you own.

  8. Build comfortable home outfits

    What can I wear at home to feel put together without a dress?

    Try a tonal set, soft trousers with an overshirt or utility coveralls. Build comfortable home outfits around movement, easy care and colors you enjoy.

  9. Try combinations beyond the obvious

    Unexpected outfit color combinations that are easy to try

    You do not need a wardrobe full of statement pieces. Start with two colors and one outfit you already like.

  10. Use deeper colors in everyday outfits

    Deep Autumn outfit ideas beyond mustard and orange

    Rich color can be quiet, colorful or casual. You have more options than a head-to-toe autumn-leaf palette.

  11. Check a purchase before paying

    Will you actually wear it? A better clothing purchase checklist

    Before buying more clothes, test the real outfit, comfort, care needs and next wearing occasion. Use this checklist to make a more useful shopping decision.

  12. Choose a pink garment for your outfit

    Does this pink fit a Winter palette? A practical shopping check

    Compare clear, icy and dusty pinks, check an online garment color and decide whether a pink top belongs in the outfits you actually wear.

  13. Plan a sun-protective outfit

    How to put together sun-protective outfits you enjoy wearing

    Build a colorful sun-protective wardrobe around coverage, UPF labels and comfortable outfit combinations, with practical ideas for a warm-weather trip.

  14. Create an ethereal look with rich colors

    Can a Winter or Autumn palette look ethereal?

    Create an ethereal outfit with navy, plum, espresso or olive. Try airy layers, flowing shapes and two worked outfits without changing your hair or eyes.

  15. Choose a light or dark cardigan

    Brown or cream cardigan? Compare the whole outfit

    Choose between a brown and cream cardigan by comparing the neckline, hem, shoes and how you wear the layer. Includes a blue-dress example and shopping checklist.

  16. Find petite trousers with usable pockets

    How to find petite trousers with pockets you can actually use

    Search for petite dress trousers and chinos with functional pockets. Use an inseam and pocket checklist, a retailer question, and a seated try-on before keeping them.

  17. Track down a discontinued garment

    How can you find a discontinued dress or sold-out piece you still want?

    Find the identifying details, separate exact matches from substitutes, and check fit before paying for the piece you missed.

  18. Build an outfit around a navy skirt

    What top goes with a navy pleated skirt? Three outfits to try

    Build three navy-skirt outfits with cream, rose or blue tops. Compare the neckline, waist and shoes before buying another piece.

  19. Choose wedding-guest color and style

    Is this dress too pale for a wedding guest? Check color and style separately

    Decide whether a pale dress fits the invitation, keep the silhouette you love and compare clearer color alternatives without buying a whole new look.

  20. Plan a work party outfit

    What to wear to a work holiday party with no dress code

    Ask one useful question, keep a familiar outfit anchor, and change the details to fit the event.
