THE STYLIST AI JOURNAL

Website Privacy

This notice applies to the Stylist AI journal at bestme.mobi/stylist_ai/blog. The app has a separate privacy policy.

Optional analytics

We load Google Analytics only if you allow it. It measures pages viewed, general traffic sources, App Store clicks and approximate article scroll progress. A click is not an installation, and scrolling does not prove someone read an article.

Google receives page paths and titles, browser and device information, and ordinary connection information. We remove query strings and fragments from page addresses; three validated campaign labels may be sent separately. External referrers are reduced to their origin. We do not collect photos or search text on this journal.

Your browser remembers your choice for up to 180 days. Analytics cookies use the sj prefix, are scoped to the journal and expire within 180 days. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled. You can withdraw consent using Privacy choices; this stops future tracking and removes the journal’s Analytics cookies, but does not erase information Google already processed.

Analytics is off until you allow it.

See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses partner-site information.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts the journal and receives ordinary connection information; see GitHub’s privacy statement. The App Store and other linked sites have their own privacy rules. App Store links include non-personal campaign labels describing the article and button.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact contact@bestme.mobi. This website does not provide a user account or ask you to upload photographs.
