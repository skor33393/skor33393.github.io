Ask whether a standalone color service is available

Define the output you need in one sentence: three outfits for a photo session, a palette for daily dressing, or a more complete professional presentation. Request an itemized scope showing which parts cover color, clothing, shopping and ongoing support. A broad package should earn its price through services you actually need, not through a vague promise of transformation.

  • Can I book color analysis on its own?
  • What deliverables and follow-up are included in the broader package?

Judge the proposal against your actual brief

For a small first step, select an existing outfit and compare one neutral and one accent on your phone. If that resolves the immediate decision, you may not need a larger consultation yet. If you need personal feedback on how the whole look fits a specific context, a live consultant offers a different kind of help. Keep color preferences separate from assumptions about competence or personality.
