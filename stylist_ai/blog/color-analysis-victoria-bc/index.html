For colour analysis in Victoria, compare the materials as well as the appointment itself. A mini session may be enough for an introduction, while a physical wallet may matter if you regularly compare fabrics in shops. The right purchase depends on how you intend to use the result afterwards.

Inspect the format before selecting the package

Ask what the mini establishes, which questions it leaves for the full consultation and whether upgrading later would duplicate the work. If you want makeup help, distinguish recommendations from an actual application or lesson. For an online appointment, ask who reviews your photos and how the discussion takes place; a human virtual consultation and an app are different services.

  • What does the mini include that I can use immediately?
  • Is the physical fabric wallet included in the package I selected?

Think about how you shop, not which package sounds fuller

Keep the first application practical: one top, one neutral and one accessory from your existing wardrobe. A physical wallet is useful beside fabric, and a digital palette is convenient when planning or browsing. Stylist AI adds photo-based exploration and everyday color tools, so compare those functions with the consultant’s deliverables instead of assuming that every digital option is the same.

A phone palette adds tools beyond a static reference

Someone who routinely compares fabrics may value a tactile wallet; someone who wants a quick reminder may be comfortable with a digital card. The full service also describes makeup application. Confirm current prices and the exact appointment inclusions directly, rather than assuming the physical wallet is offered in every format.

Stylist AI is an interactive digital route. Begin with a clear selfie and use its seasonal palette, outfit combinations, makeup and hair previews to explore possibilities. Camera-based colour comparison adds another way to consider garments. The app does not provide fabric samples or an analyst’s explanation of drapes. Choose the consultation for those components, or begin with the app if repeated independent exploration is your main need.
