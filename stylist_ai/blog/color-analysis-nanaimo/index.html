QUESTIONS FOR A SALON-BASED CONSULTATION

Nanaimo colour analysis in a salon: clarify what the hour includes

Colours with Krysta’s indexed service information describes a 60-minute, 16-category consultation at KG Hair Salon. Its setting makes one question especially important: which services are part of that hour?

Does the colour analysis include a hair treatment?

The reviewed description establishes a consultation with clothing, makeup and jewellery guidance. A treatment should be quoted separately unless the provider explicitly includes it.

Is the current fee confirmed?

No. The indexed service description and analyst directory support the lead; request a current booking quote directly.

Separate personal colour guidance from a salon hair service

Nanaimo readers may encounter colour analysis within a hair-salon setting. That can make it easy to connect palette ideas with a future hair discussion, but it does not mean that the analysis fee includes colouring or styling. Ask for the scope of each appointment separately.

Ask how the 16 categories become practical advice

If hair is your main reason for booking, bring a picture of your current colour and a few examples you like. Ask whether the analysis also covers clothing neutrals and makeup, what reference you keep, and whether the hair consultation needs another slot. A palette category should be the beginning of a conversation about the look you want.

  • Is the consultation separate from hair colouring and styling?
  • What palette or written reference will I receive?

Bring your existing jewellery into the discussion

Use a preview to compare broad directions before making a salon decision, then let the stylist assess the real hair and maintenance requirements. For your wardrobe, start with a familiar outfit and one alternative near-face colour. Stylist AI can keep that exploration on your phone while you decide whether hands-on guidance would be helpful.

A preview is useful before a separate salon decision

The number of categories does not tell you how much time you will have for questions or which materials you leave with. Ask whether the appointment can address the two decisions most relevant to you—for example, clothing neutrals and jewellery—and whether makeup advice is a discussion, application or lesson.
