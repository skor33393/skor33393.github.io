Three details to settle before paying a deposit

Ask whether the appointment time leaves enough daylight for the provider’s process, what preparation is expected, and how much an optional physical palette costs. If stairs are a problem, discuss access before paying a deposit. Bring a photo of the jacket or outfit that prompted the booking, so the consultation can connect the palette to a real decision.

  • Is the introductory rate still available for my date?
  • What is the total with a physical palette, and is the studio accessible to me?

If the stairs or timing rule out a studio visit

In-person route

Check the upstairs access, appointment time and current introductory rate. Add the physical palette price only if you want that item.

The provider’s remote route

The same site describes virtual analysis from approved photos, with results within 30 days of photo approval. That is a different timetable from attending a session.

Ask for the current remote price and photo requirements instead of applying the studio fee to the virtual service. For a phone-based, self-guided route, compare the current Stylist AI plan and the tools you will use. Write down which output you need: a conversation, physical materials or a palette you can revisit.

Test the palette on a layer you already own

Afterwards, choose one everyday layer and one accent to test together. Outdoorsy clothes, office clothes and evening clothes can all use the same color relationship without needing the same fabric or formality. The app is useful for keeping that idea available on your phone; a physical swatch is useful when examining the actual garment. Compare in even light rather than treating a retail display spotlight as a reliable reference.

Do you need fabric in your hand or ideas on your phone?

A physical swatch can travel from the consultation into a fabric comparison; an app screen cannot replace the material’s texture or finish. That makes the optional wallet a more relevant extra for some people than a long list of new clothes. Ask to see the wallet before deciding whether to buy it.

Stylist AI offers a separate route: choose your interests in the introduction, add a clear selfie, then explore a palette, outfit combinations and makeup or hair previews. You can revisit those tools without arranging studio access. Use the camera color check to consider an existing layer, while judging the actual fabric yourself. Premium features are offered through in-app purchases, so compare the current plan with the complete studio quote.
