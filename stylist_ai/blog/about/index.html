THE STYLIST AI JOURNAL

About the Stylist AI Journal

This journal is published by Aleksandr Korenev, the developer of Stylist AI for iPhone. We answer practical questions about color analysis, palettes and everyday styling.

Questions first

Public questions help us choose topics. We write original answers, link to the discussion when useful, and do not reproduce people’s selfies or imply they use our app. Community votes show engagement, not proof that an opinion is correct.

How we write

We distinguish styling frameworks from measurable photography facts, link to relevant sources, and label our own practical suggestions. Guides may be prepared with AI assistance and are checked against the cited sources and verified product information. We do not claim professional color-consultant credentials or independent expert review.

Our relationship with Stylist AI

We make the app and benefit from subscriptions. Articles include clearly identified app links, alongside steps you can try without a purchase. Photos and camera conditions affect results; palettes and scores do not prove attractiveness or require you to follow a rule.

Illustrations

The outfit artwork is an illustration created for Stylist AI. Color strips are illustrative examples, not a calibrated reference or a personal analysis.

Corrections

Send the article URL and your correction to contact@bestme.mobi. Publication dates reflect publication; modification dates change when the content materially changes.
