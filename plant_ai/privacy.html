Privacy & product preview

Plant Doctor AI is a product concept being tested. The scans and care plans shown are illustrative. The app is not yet available to download. A store or care-plan button opens our launch signup form. The app is coming soon; clicking a store button does not install anything or charge you. You can choose to leave your email and ask us to notify you when the app launches.

Usage measurement

This site uses Google Analytics to understand visits, campaign sources, page engagement, feature interest, and App Store or Google Play button taps. Google Analytics may use browser cookies to recognize visits. We do not request photos, payment information, or precise location. If you join the launch list, we collect the email address you enter separately from Google Analytics.

We disable Google advertising personalization and Google signals. We send a cleaned page URL with campaign tags and only the referring website’s origin. Google processes measurement data under its privacy policy. Hosting infrastructure may process request logs to deliver the site.

TikTok advertising measurement

We use TikTok Pixel to measure page views, store-button interest and completed launch-list signups, and to attribute these actions to TikTok ads. TikTok may receive page URLs, campaign and click identifiers, browser/device information, IP address and cookie identifiers. The pixel’s Automatic Advanced Matching setting can also match hashed contact information entered into forms, such as an email address, to TikTok users for advertising attribution. We do not send email addresses to Google Analytics. TikTok processes this information under its Partner Privacy Policy.

Launch notifications

When you select “Notify me at launch,” you ask us to email you about the Plant Doctor AI launch. We store your email, signup time, consent, store preference, landing-page variant and campaign labels in our dedicated Firebase database in the United States. Only authorized project operators can read the signup list; website visitors cannot read, change or delete list entries. We use your email for launch notifications and do not send it to Google Analytics.

Your choice

A browser Global Privacy Control signal disables both Google Analytics and TikTok Pixel. You can also disable both services for future visits in this browser below. This choice is saved locally and does not submit a form.
