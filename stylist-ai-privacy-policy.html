Photos & face data

What face data we process

When you use a feature involving a face, Stylist AI processes the photograph you take or select and may create cropped images of facial areas, including the eyes.

The app also processes facial landmarks, measurements, and other information derived from the photo. Depending on the feature, this includes skin tone and undertone, hair and eye colors, face shape, facial proportions, contrast, and color-season recommendations. Some features generate facial-proportion scores, explanatory comments, and visual overlays.

We treat these photographs and the associated facial information and results as face data.

How we use face data

We use face data to provide the features you request: personal color analysis, style and makeup recommendations, facial-proportion visualizations, virtual makeup, and saved analysis results and preferences.

We do not sell face data or use it to target advertising. We do not use submitted face data to train AI models or authorize our processing providers to use it for that purpose.

Optional 3D face scan (ARKit)

On supported devices, you can choose a guided face scan instead of uploading a selfie. This feature uses Apple’s ARKit face tracking and the front camera, including TrueDepth capabilities where available. It processes camera frames, facial geometry and landmarks, head position and movement, eye direction, and eyelid openness. We use these signals to guide your position and head movement, display the scan overlay, and select a clear photo for your requested analysis.

The ARKit face mesh, facial landmark coordinates, raw head-pose and eye-direction values, and expression coefficients are processed on your device. The face mesh is held in memory for the scan and the analysis animation; it is not saved as a 3D model or uploaded to our backend or AI providers. Unselected camera frames are not saved as a video recording. Temporary scan data is released when the relevant processing objects are released or the app ends.

The selected camera photo follows the same cloud-analysis process as an uploaded selfie. The app may also send cropped eye images and sampled color information to Google Firebase / Google Cloud and OpenAI, as described below. The cloud color analysis uses those photographic inputs, not the ARKit 3D mesh. Saved photos, avatars, visualizations, and analysis results follow the storage and deletion rules below.

The scan is a capture aid for your style analysis. It does not authenticate you and does not access your Face ID enrollment or authentication data. The scan is optional: you can cancel it or choose Upload a selfie. On devices that do not support ARKit face tracking, the scan option is not offered.

Face-data sharing and third-party protection

Some facial measurements and visual processing take place on your device to support the app’s features. Cloud analysis uses the separate processing steps described below.

Google Firebase / Google Cloud. For cloud analysis, the app sends the selected photograph and relevant analysis inputs to our Google-hosted backend. Inputs may include cropped eye images, sampled colors, and existing appearance information needed for the requested feature. Google provides backend processing and server logging.

OpenAI. Our backend sends the relevant images and inputs to OpenAI to generate the requested analysis or recommendation. The result is returned to the app through our backend.

We share face data with Google Firebase / Google Cloud and OpenAI to provide the cloud analysis you request. We do not sell or rent face data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

We require every third party receiving face data, including Google Firebase / Google Cloud and OpenAI, to provide the same or equal protection of that data as described in this Privacy Policy and required by Apple’s App Review Guidelines. Providers may process face data only for the disclosed service and associated security and legal obligations, subject to the safeguards and retention and deletion practices described here.

Information about General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

We may be collecting and using information from you if you are from the European Economic Area (EEA), and in this section of our Privacy Policy we are going to explain exactly how and why is this data collected, and how we maintain this data under protection from being replicated or used in the wrong way.

What is GDPR?

GDPR is an EU-wide privacy and data protection law that regulates how EU residents' data is protected by companies and enhances the control the EU residents have, over their personal data.

The GDPR is relevant to any globally operating company and not just the EU-based businesses and EU residents. Our customers’ data is important irrespective of where they are located, which is why we have implemented GDPR controls as our baseline standard for all our operations worldwide.

What is personal data?

Any data that relates to an identifiable or identified individual. GDPR covers a broad spectrum of information that could be used on its own, or in combination with other pieces of information, to identify a person. Personal data extends beyond a person’s name or email address. Some examples include financial information, political opinions, genetic data, biometric data, IP addresses, physical address, sexual orientation, and ethnicity.

The Data Protection Principles include requirements such as:

  • Personal data collected must be processed in a fair, legal, and transparent way and should only be used in a way that a person would reasonably expect.
  • Personal data should only be collected to fulfil a specific purpose and it should only be used for that purpose. Organizations must specify why they need the personal data when they collect it.
  • Personal data should be held no longer than necessary to fulfil its purpose.
  • People covered by the GDPR have the right to access their own personal data. They can also request a copy of their data, and that their data be updated, deleted, restricted, or moved to another organization.

Why is GDPR important?

GDPR adds some new requirements regarding how companies should protect individuals' personal data that they collect and process. It also raises the stakes for compliance by increasing enforcement and imposing greater fines for breach. Beyond these facts it's simply the right thing to do. At Stylist AI we strongly believe that your data privacy is very important and we already have solid security and privacy practices in place that go beyond the requirements of this new regulation.
