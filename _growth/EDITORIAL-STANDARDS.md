# Durable SEO and editorial practices

Approved by the user on 2026-10-31 after the Claude SEO audit. Apply to all future Stylist AI and Pelvic Gym articles and substantial updates. These practices complement RUNBOOK.md; daily targets stay five distinct question intents per app at 22:00 Asia/Dubai.

## Before writing

Record four fields in each internal brief: (1) the reader's concrete decision or task, (2) the best existing destination and overlap decision, (3) a useful original example, demonstration, worksheet or verified piece of evidence, and (4) exactly how the app helps. Public forum attention is topic evidence, not keyword volume. Keep source questions and engagement records internal.

Serve an existing intent by materially improving its page. Create a new URL only for a distinct useful task. Never pad to a fixed word count, invent firsthand testing, copy competitor wording/images, or fill a daily quota with near-duplicates. Preserve publication dates; update modification/source dates only when warranted.

## Show useful evidence

For visual styling tasks, show the comparison with labeled colors, an original diagram, real licensed captures or the current app workflow. Decorative cover art does not substitute for the substantive example. Clearly distinguish a schematic or demo profile from an actual measured experiment; never describe ad fixtures as new analysis. Respect the user's existing product copy/model preferences and do not add unsolicited AI/approximate-color captions.

Health guidance requires checked authoritative clinical sources and assessment-first scope for pain, tension, emptying difficulties or surgical restrictions. Do not invent authors, credentials, clinician approval, efficacy evidence or diagnostic features. As of October 31, no independent clinician is engaged. The original draft bladder diary remains outside the public repository until qualified review. A source's review date does not establish that our article was clinically reviewed.

Make the next step usable: a decision checklist, comparison table, worked example or appropriate existing official resource. A reader should get value without installing. Tailor the app CTA to the actual task; a store click is not an install.

## Navigation, images and presentation

Add meaningful incoming contextual links from relevant existing articles when publishing. Use the regular Stylist topic hubs and existing Pelvic categories; avoid arbitrary link quotas. City guides remain excluded from regular journal grids, main navigation and homepage ItemLists. The user approved the plan's contextual route from the professional-color-analysis comparison to the separate city directory. Do not add city promotional panels or restore the rejected top city-directory link.

Keep image-first city banners fully tappable, with official Apple badges and Safari Smart App Banners. Keep the compact mobile local-details shortcut outside the store link. Use Asian models for Asian-market city pages and the approved American model for US/Canada pages. Distinct city wording/layout is insufficient by itself: use verified local decision evidence, explicitly narrow coverage where appropriate, and no invented local studios, reviews or Business Profiles.

Before adding assets, run check_capacity.py; preserve the 800 MB stop threshold. Save compressed production assets only, normally under 250 KB. Run prepare_images.py with the bundled Python/Pillow runtime before building to create 400/800-width cover variants. Retain lazy cards, eager primary images, alt text and intrinsic dimensions. Never change the active advertising landing or shared assets.

## Verify and measure

Run the existing build, validators, analytics tests and relevant city checks. Inspect representative mobile/desktop layouts where available; report unavailable checks honestly. Verify deployed pages and new assets, not just a push. Sitemaps/HTTP 200 do not prove indexing.

Maintain page cohorts and compare equal reported periods. Separate discovered/crawled/indexed states, non-brand impressions/clicks, consented reading and store clicks. Do not infer a trend from tiny samples or invent performance scores when measurement fails. Review a small pilot before expanding a tactic. No promised ranking position, fake keyword volumes, paid-link scheme or automated forum promotion.
