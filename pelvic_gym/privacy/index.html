Website privacy · October 24, 2026

A quieter kind of website.

This notice applies to the Pelvic Gym journal at bestme.mobi/pelvic_gym. The app has a separate privacy policy.

No website analytics tracker is active

This journal currently has no Google Analytics, advertising pixels, session recordings, or third-party visitor analytics script. Its JavaScript does not set cookies or store search terms. The guide filters and search operate in your browser.

Hosting and ordinary web requests

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading a page involves sending ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to the hosting infrastructure. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of that information.

Links to the App Store and health resources

When you follow an external link, that service’s privacy rules apply. App Store links include campaign labels identifying the journal page and button location, not your identity. Those labels do not tell us whether you installed the app. Health-source links are provided for your reference.

Contacting us

If you email contact@bestme.mobi, your email provider transmits your message and address. Please avoid sending private medical information; this inbox does not provide healthcare.

Future changes

If we connect an analytics service, we will update this notice to explain the service and the information collected.
